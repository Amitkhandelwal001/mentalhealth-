## Getting Started

### What is MindCare?

MindCare is a comprehensive mental health platform designed to help you:
- Track your daily mood patterns
- Receive personalized activity recommendations
- Connect with a supportive community
- Access crisis resources when needed
- Maintain consistent wellness habits through gamification

### System Requirements

- Modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- Internet connection
- JavaScript enabled
- Recommended: Desktop or mobile device with screen size 320px+

## Authentication

### Creating an Account

1. Visit the MindCare homepage
2. Click "Sign Up" or "Get Started"
3. Fill in your information:
   - **Username**: 3-20 characters, will be used for anonymous community interactions
   - **Email**: Your valid email address
   - **Password**: At least 6 characters (recommendation: use a strong password)
4. Click "Register"
5. You'll be automatically logged in after successful registration

### Logging In

1. Click "Login" from the homepage
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click "Sign In"
4. You'll be redirected to your dashboard

### Account Security

- Your password is securely hashed and stored
- You'll remain logged in for 30 days unless you log out
- If you suspect unauthorized access, log out and change your password

## Mood Tracking

### Daily Mood Check-ins

**Purpose**: Track your emotional state over time to identify patterns and triggers.

**How to use**:
1. Navigate to "Mood Check-in" from the main menu
2. Select your mood on the 1-5 scale:
   - 😢 **1**: Very sad/down
   - 😕 **2**: Somewhat down
   - 😐 **3**: Neutral/okay
   - 🙂 **4**: Good/happy
   - 😊 **5**: Very happy/excellent
3. Choose the time of day:
   - **Morning**: Before noon
   - **Afternoon**: Noon to 6 PM
   - **Evening**: After 6 PM
4. **Optional**: Add notes about your mood
5. **Optional**: Add tags (e.g., "work", "family", "exercise")
6. Click "Save Mood Entry"

### Mood History

**Viewing your history**:
1. Go to "Mood History" from the main menu
2. View your entries by:
   - Most recent first
   - Filtered by date range
   - Grouped by time of day
3. Edit or delete entries by clicking the action buttons

### Understanding Mood Patterns

- **Streaks**: Consecutive days with mood entries
- **Trends**: Your mood patterns over time
- **Time Patterns**: How your mood varies by time of day
- **Averages**: Your typical mood levels

## Activities

### What are Activities?

Activities are wellness exercises designed to improve your mental health based on your current mood and needs.

### Activity Categories

**🫁 Breathing Exercises**:
- Deep breathing techniques
- Box breathing
- 4-7-8 breathing method
- Progressive muscle relaxation

**📝 Journaling**:
- Gratitude journaling
- Emotion processing
- Daily reflection prompts
- Goal setting exercises

**🏃 Physical Activities**:
- Gentle stretching
- Walking meditation
- Yoga poses
- Dance movement

**🧘 Mindfulness**:
- Guided meditation
- Body scan exercises
- Mindful observation
- Loving-kindness practice

### How to Use Activities

1. **Browse Activities**:
   - Visit the "Activities" page
   - Filter by category or difficulty level
   - Read activity descriptions

2. **Get Recommendations**:
   - Based on your recent mood entries
   - Personalized suggestions appear on your dashboard
   - Recommendations update as your patterns change

3. **Complete an Activity**:
   - Click "Start Activity"
   - Follow the step-by-step instructions
   - Take your time - no pressure
   - Rate the activity when finished (1-5 stars)
   - Add optional notes about your experience

4. **Track Progress**:
   - View your activity history
   - See which activities work best for you
   - Earn points for completion

## Community

### Anonymous Support System

**Purpose**: Connect with others facing similar challenges while maintaining complete privacy.

### Community Features

**📝 Posts**:
- Share your experiences anonymously
- Choose from categories:
  - **Support**: Seeking help or encouragement
  - **Celebration**: Sharing victories and positive moments
  - **Question**: Asking for advice or information
  - **General**: Open discussion and thoughts

**💬 Comments**:
- Respond to posts with support and encouragement
- Share your own experiences
- Offer practical advice

**❤️ Interactions**:
- Like posts and comments that resonate with you
- Save posts for later reference
- Report inappropriate content

### Community Guidelines

**Do**:
- Be supportive and encouraging
- Share authentically
- Respect others' experiences
- Offer constructive advice
- Use content warnings for sensitive topics

**Don't**:
- Share personal identifying information
- Give medical advice
- Use harmful language
- Spam or advertise
- Share crisis content without proper resources

### Creating Posts

1. Click "Create Post" in the community section
2. Choose a category
3. Write a descriptive title
4. Share your thoughts in the content area
5. Add relevant tags (optional)
6. Click "Post Anonymously"

### Privacy Protection

- All posts are completely anonymous
- Your username is never shown
- Anonymous names are generated (e.g., "Anonymous Butterfly")
- No personal information is ever shared

## Crisis Support

### When to Use Crisis Resources

If you're experiencing:
- Thoughts of self-harm
- Suicidal ideation
- Severe mental health crisis
- Immediate danger to yourself or others

### Automatic Detection

MindCare automatically detects crisis-related keywords in:
- Mood entry notes
- Community posts
- Comments

When detected, you'll immediately see crisis resources and support information.

### Crisis Resources

**🇺🇸 United States**:
- **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline**: Call or text 988
- **Crisis Text Line**: Text HOME to 741741
- **National Domestic Violence Hotline**: 1-800-799-7233

**🌍 International**:
- **UK - Samaritans**: 116 123
- **Canada - Talk Suicide**: 1-833-456-4566
- **Australia - Lifeline**: 13 11 14
- **India - AASRA**: +91-9820466726

**🆘 Emergency**: Call 911 (US) or your local emergency number

### Get Help Page

Access comprehensive crisis resources at any time:
1. Click "🆘 Get Help" in the main navigation
2. Find immediate crisis resources
3. Access mental health services
4. Learn warning signs
5. Get support for helping others

## Tips for Success

### Establishing Healthy Habits

1. **Consistency Over Perfection**:
   - Aim for daily mood check-ins
   - Don't worry about missed days
   - Focus on building the habit

2. **Honest Self-Reflection**:
   - Be truthful about your mood
   - Include notes about what affected your mood
   - Track patterns over time

3. **Engage with Activities**:
   - Try different types of activities
   - Rate activities honestly
   - Revisit activities that work for you

4. **Community Participation**:
   - Share your experiences
   - Support others in their journey
   - Read posts for inspiration and tips

### Making the Most of MindCare

**Weekly Routine**:
- Monday: Review last week's mood patterns
- Daily: Complete mood check-in
- 3x/week: Try recommended activities
- Weekly: Engage with community posts

**Monthly Review**:
- Check your mood trends
- Celebrate achievements
- Adjust your wellness strategies
- Set new goals

### Setting Realistic Goals

- Start with small, achievable targets
- Celebrate small victories
- Be patient with progress

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Login Problems**:
- Check your email and password
- Ensure internet connection
- Clear browser cache
- Try incognito/private browsing

**Mood Tracking Issues**:
- Refresh the page
- Check that you've selected all required fields
- Ensure you haven't already logged a mood for that time of day

**Activity Loading Problems**:
- Check internet connection
- Refresh the page
- Try a different browser
- Clear browser cache

**Community Features Not Working**:
- Verify you're logged in
- Check internet connection
- Ensure your post meets community guidelines
- Try refreshing the page

### Browser Compatibility

**Supported Browsers**:
- Chrome 70+
- Firefox 65+
- Safari 12+
- Edge 79+

**Features requiring JavaScript**:
- All interactive elements
- Charts and graphs
- Real-time updates
- Form submissions

### Getting Help

**Technical Issues**:
- Check the troubleshooting section

- Try a different browser
- Contact support if problems persist

**Account Issues**:

### Privacy and Security

**Account Security**:
